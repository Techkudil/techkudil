Let's Check Our Best Services In City

At Techkudil, we provide reliable, fast, and expert mobile repair services for all major brands like Samsung, Vivo, Oppo, Redmi, iPhone, Realme, Poco, OnePlus, and more. Whether it’s a simple issue or a complex chip-level fault, we ensure professional repair with high-quality parts and transparent communication.

Display Replacement

Broken or non-working display? We offer original and premium-grade display replacements for all smartphone models. Our technicians ensure perfect touch response, accurate colors, and factory-level fitting.

Battery Replacement

If your phone drains fast, heats up, or switches off suddenly, it's time for a battery change. We provide long-lasting OEM batteries for all brands with same-day delivery.

Charging Port Repair

Slow charging or not charging at all? We fix charging port issues, loose connectors, and Type-C/USB problems with high precision soldering.

Speaker & Mic Issue Repair

Low sound? Voice not clear? We clean, repair, or replace mic, loudspeaker, earpiece speaker, and audio IC faults.

Water Damage Treatment

Accidentally spilled water on your phone? We provide deep cleaning, corrosion removal, and chip-level diagnosis to recover your mobile safely.

Software Issues & OS Reinstall

We fix bootloop, hanging, slow performance, FRP lock, OS corruption, and app issues. We also provide OS reinstallation and updates.

Motherboard / Chip-Level Repair

We handle advanced motherboard issues including no power or dead phones, auto restart problems, display light issues, Charging IC and Power IC faults, U2 IC, Network IC, Audio IC, and Touch IC repairs—perfect for bringing back devices that others cannot fix.

Camera Repair & Replacement

Blurry photos? Camera not opening? We repair front/back camera issues, replace broken lenses, and fix camera IC problems.

Network & SIM Issue Repair

No signal? SIM not detecting? We solve network IC issues, SIM tray faults, antenna problems, and baseband errors.

Power Button & Volume Button Repair

Non-working or stuck buttons? We repair hardware switches, flex cables, and internal connectors.

Mobile Heating Issue Fix

Overheating due to battery, apps, or motherboard? We identify the root cause and provide proper solutions.

Data Recovery

Accidentally deleted files? Mobile dead? We provide data backup and secure recovery services.

Fingerprint & Face Unlock Repair

We repair fingerprint sensors, face unlock issues, and calibration errors.

Complete Mobile Servicing

For overall performance, we offer complete servicing including cleaning, software optimization, and hardware check.
