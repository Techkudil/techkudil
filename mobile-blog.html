Top 5 Mobile Repair Issues We Fix Daily at Techkudil

1. Broken or Cracked Display – Most Common Repair

Cracked screens are the number one issue customers bring to Techkudil. A simple slip from your hand or pocket can shatter the screen, affecting both touchscreen response and visibility. At Techkudil, we provide fast and reliable display replacement for all brands such as Samsung, Vivo, Oppo, Redmi, Realme, Apple, and more. We use premium-grade screens, ensuring original-like clarity, touch sensitivity, and long life. Most display changes are completed within 30–45 minutes.

2. Battery Draining Fast / Not Charging Properly

Another frequent issue is poor battery performance. Over time, phone batteries weaken, resulting in fast draining, sudden shutdowns, or overheating.Our experts diagnose whether the problem is due to the battery, charging IC, adapter, or charging port, and replace the defective part with high-quality components. After repair, your mobile regains excellent backup and smooth performance.

3. Charging Port Not Working

Dust, moisture, or repeated cable usage can damage the charging port. When this happens, your phone may charge slowly, disconnect frequently, or not charge at all.Techkudil offers charging port cleaning, soldering, and port replacement based on your mobile model. We use micro-soldering tools to ensure the charging point works exactly like new.

4. Software Issues / Boot Loop / Hang Problems

Many customers visit us for mobile software problems such as:Mobile stuck on logo,Auto restart,Slow performance,Virus/malware issues,App crashes,Forgotten password.Techkudil provides professional software flashing, OS installation, data backup, reset services, and virus removal, making your phone run smoother and faster instantly.

5. Speaker, Mic & Camera Problems

Daily wear and tear, dust, or accidental damage can affect the audio and camera functions.We fix:Ear speaker voice low,Mic not working,Loudspeaker issues,Camera blur,Camera not opening. Our technicians repair or replace only the required parts, ensuring your mobile’s audio and camera quality returns to perfect condition.
