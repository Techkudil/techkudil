The information collected by Techkudil is used solely for customer service improvement, service booking confirmation, troubleshooting support, product recommendations, PlayStation repair updates, order communication, and website enhancement. Your data helps us offer better repair accuracy, quicker turnaround time, and personalized product suggestions. We do not misuse your data for unnecessary marketing, and we never compromise your confidentiality. Techkudil’s goal is to provide a smarter, smoother, and more secure shopping and service experience for every customer.

Techkudil does not sell, rent, or trade customer data to any third-party companies. We maintain full control over how your data is handled and ensure every partner follows safe data protection standards. Under legal requirements, we may disclose information only if demanded by law enforcement agencies, ensuring complete compliance without compromising customer trust.

Data Security & Protection

Techkudil implements strict security protocols, encrypted systems, secure servers, and access-controlled environments to ensure all customer information remains safe. We regularly monitor our website for vulnerabilities, unauthorized access attempts, malware risks, and fraudulent activity. Payment information is handled through secure payment gateways and never stored directly by us. Every communication, inquiry, and repair detail is processed through safe internal systems, ensuring complete protection for customers across Tamil Nadu. Your trust is our priority, and we continuously enhance our systems to meet modern cybersecurity standards.
