These terms help protect both customers and our technicians, ensuring smooth service, clear communication, and safe transactions. Techkudil is committed to offering reliable electronic repair solutions, original accessories, and trusted customer support across Tamil Nadu.

Service Policy

All repairs carried out at Techkudil—including mobile service, laptop service, motherboard chip-level repair, and PlayStation console service—are performed by trained technicians using high-quality parts. Device condition will be inspected before service, and customers will be informed of estimated costs, repair time, and risks (if any). Once the repair is approved, Techkudil is not responsible for unrelated internal failures or pre-existing issues. Water-damage, motherboard-level faults, and unstable devices may require repeated testing, and repair success depends on device condition.

Product Warranty

Techkudil provides warranty only for the parts replaced or accessories purchased through our shop. Warranty duration varies depending on the product, with display replacements, batteries, and accessories covered under specific terms explained during purchase. No warranty is provided for water-damaged devices, motherboard-level repairs, or physical damage after service. Warranty becomes void if the device is opened, repaired elsewhere, or used improperly.

Order & Booking Policy

Techkudil ensures that all brand-new, refurbished, and pre-owned products—such as smartphones, laptops, desktop units, PlayStation consoles, and gaming accessories—are tested and verified before sale. Customers will receive complete product details including model, condition, storage, and available warranty. Once sold, returns are accepted only for manufacturing defects covered under warranty; otherwise, sales are considered final.

Return & Refund Policy

Refunds or returns at Techkudil are processed only if a valid warranty issue is identified and approved by our support team. Damage caused by customer mishandling, drops, liquid exposure, or unauthorized repair will not qualify for returns. For accessories and electronic components, once opened or used, returns are not permitted. Refund processing time may vary based on payment method and verification.

Payment Terms

Techkudil accepts multiple payment methods including UPI, cash, net banking, and card payments. For service-related work, customers may be asked to pay an advance before repair begins. Full payment must be completed before delivery of the repaired device or purchased product. Any disputes related to payments will be handled through Techkudil’s customer support team following standard procedures.

Privacy & Data Handling

At Techkudil, we respect customer privacy and ensure that personal data, device contents, photos, documents, and login details remain confidential during service. Customers are advised to back up their data before submitting devices, as Techkudil is not responsible for data loss caused by hardware issues.

Limitation of Liability

Techkudil is not responsible for hidden internal defects, fragile components, or issues that arise due to previous repair attempts, counterfeit parts, or device age. Devices with liquid damage, motherboard corrosion, or power IC faults may fail during repair attempts due to unstable condition. While we follow the best repair practices, final outcomes depend on the actual hardware condition.

Customer Responsibility

Customers must provide accurate device information, report previous repair history, and pick up devices within the given time. Customers are also responsible for verifying device functionality at the time of delivery.
